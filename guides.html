How we hire

Every guide, every season

A guide here spends their first season as a boatman on Browns Canyon before they take a paddle boat of their own, and re-certifies every spring. Nobody guides a stretch they have not run that season.

River ratings, plainly

We use the international scale. It is a description of the water, not of you — but the two meet at the minimum age on each trip.

    Class I–II

    Moving water, small waves, obstacles you can see in time. You can steer or not.

    Class III

    Rapids with irregular waves and a clear channel. You paddle as a crew and you will get wet.

    Class IV

    Powerful, technical water where a mistake costs a swim. Scouting from the bank is routine.

    Class V

    Long, violent rapids with real consequence. Our Gorge trips reach this at high water, and we screen for it.
