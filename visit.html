Driving in

Directions

    From Denver · 2h 50m

    US-285 south to Fairplay, then US-24 south to Buena Vista, then US-24/US-50 south into Salida. We are on the right, two miles past the last light.

    From Colorado Springs · 2h 10m

    US-24 west through Eleven Mile and Hartsel to Buena Vista, then US-24 south to Salida. Same two miles past the last light.

    From the south · 1h 20m

    US-285 north to Poncha Springs, then US-50 east into Salida. We are on the left, before the town.

    No car

    There is no public transit to the put-ins. If you are riding in on the bus, call us and we will tell you what is realistically walkable and what is not.

On site

What is here

  • Changing rooms and hot showers
  • Lockers for the day, free with your trip
  • Parking, including for the shuttle van and trailers
  • A gear shop: PFDs, helmets, wetsuits, straps, sunscreen
  • Coffee from 6:30 am, and a fill station for water bottles
  • A covered picnic area you can use before or after a trip
  • Dog water bowls and shade — dogs ride free on the float
  • An accessible restroom and an accessible route to the launch point

If you need a step-free route to the water or adaptive equipment, call, text or email before you book. We will tell you what we can rig and what we cannot, before you drive here.
