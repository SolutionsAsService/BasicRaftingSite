The gauge is the whole business

Read this before you pick a date

The Arkansas is free-flowing: no dam above Salida, so the water is whatever the snowpack and the weather say it is. Each trip below carries the flow window it can be guided in — a low end and a high end, on the same scale as the gauge.

At the sample reading of 2,480 cfs (11 Jun 2026), 5 of 6 trips would run and Pine Creek would be closed: it needs the river to drop before it can be guided.

Move the gauge to see it change, or jump to two readings worth seeing: 900 cfs, where all 6 run, and 5,200 cfs, where 1 does.

Before you book

Which one is yours?

First time on whitewater

Start with Browns Canyon. It is class III: real rapids, named and continuous, with a pool after each one to catch your breath. Minimum age 8.

Young kids, or someone who would rather watch

The Buena Vista Float is six miles of flat water with a few riffles. Minimum age 3, dogs ride free, and nobody has to paddle if they do not want to.

You have paddled before

The Numbers is twelve miles of class III–IV with no let-up, and Pine Creek is five miles of class IV that runs best when the rest of the river is dropping. Both will be the hardest water you have guided.

The biggest day we sell

Royal Gorge: class IV–V under the highest bridge in North America. We ask about your experience before we confirm, and we turn people away. Minimum age 16.
